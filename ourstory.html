OUR STORY

Warm hospitality,
rooted in tradition.

Ziyafat was created to bring the soul of Afghan dining to a place where stories, laughter, and food become one shared experience.

SINCE 2012

We cook for connection.

Every dish at Ziyafat is shaped by the way Afghan families gather: with generosity, patience, and a deep sense of welcome. From the first greeting to the final dessert, every moment is designed to feel personal.

OUR VALUES

Freshness, family, and flavour.

We use thoughtfully sourced ingredients, slow-cooked methods, and a warm, attentive approach that turns meals into memories.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Good food brings people together.

Whether it is a small breakfast table or a celebratory banquet, we treat every guest like part of the family.

2012

Our journey began

With a single vision: to serve real Afghan flavours in a welcoming setting.

2018

Expanded the table

We grew from a neighbourhood favourite into a destination for gatherings.

Today

Celebrating every occasion

From everyday meals to weddings and corporate dinners, we serve with heart.
